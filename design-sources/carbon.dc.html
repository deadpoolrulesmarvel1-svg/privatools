Methodology

A claim is only marked verified when it has a source URL, a checked date and a recorded currency. Until then it reads “No evidence supplied”, never a date. Priority order changes which rows lead — it never produces a score, a crown or a ranking. Where a competitor is genuinely stronger we will say so, with the source. We publish no ratings, votes or testimonials.
